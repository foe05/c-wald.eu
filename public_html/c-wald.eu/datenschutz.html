2. Contact form

If you submit the contact form on this website, the data you enter (name, organisation, email address, subject, message) will be transmitted to us by email and stored for the purpose of processing your inquiry and for possible follow-up questions.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to inquiries). If your inquiry concerns the initiation of a contract, the additional legal basis is Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR.

Storage duration: Data from contact inquiries are deleted once the matter has been finally resolved, unless statutory retention obligations require longer storage.

Spam protection: We use a hidden honeypot field to filter automated submissions. No data from legitimate users is recorded by this mechanism.

Our hosting provider processes data on our behalf based on a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR. This data is processed on the basis of Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and stable operation of the website) and deleted after a short period.

4. Reach measurement (server-side event counting)

To understand how often our pages are used, we count a small number of anonymous events on the server side. No cookies, no localStorage entries, and no JavaScript trackers are used for this. Because no information is stored on or read from your device, this processing is not subject to § 25 TDDDG.

The following events are counted:

  • opening of the start page (c-wald.eu/),
  • opening of the Rostock page (c-wald.eu/rostock/),
  • a successful submission of the Rostock waitlist form.

Each event transmits only the fact that it occurred, together with a fixed site identifier and version. No personal data is included — in particular no IP address, no device or browser information, and none of the data you enter into any form. The events are identical for every visitor and cannot be linked to an individual.

Events are transmitted to log.broetzens.de, operated by the same controller as c-wald.eu.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in measuring reach and improving the website).

5. Cookies

We use only strictly necessary technical cookies and localStorage entries required to operate the site (e.g. to remember your cookie consent preference). These do not require consent under § 25 (2) TDDDG.

No analytics, tracking, or marketing cookies are currently active. Should this change in the future, consent will be obtained in advance via our consent banner. (Reach measurement is performed server-side without cookies — see section 4.)

6. Fonts (Google Fonts)

This site loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited). Connecting to Google servers transmits your IP address. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in consistent typographic presentation). [PLACEHOLDER: Consider self-hosting fonts to avoid this transfer.]

8. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority — in particular in the Member State of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement — if you believe that the processing of your personal data infringes the GDPR (Art. 77 GDPR).
